# Disclosure — 2026-09-28 (extended 2026-10-07)

**May 19 primaries (locked in this registry on 2026-05-09).** The Kentucky Senate, Alabama Senate and Georgia Governor Republican primary forecasts were produced by PULSE INTEL prototype build v48.1 with polling at zero weight. The inputs that carried weight — fundamentals, expert ratings and sentiment — were simulated values generated by hash functions of the race ID. The retained v48.1 build reproduces all three locked figures exactly. They are not valid model forecasts, and the weights, methodology version and accuracy figures published with them are withdrawn. The locked records are unchanged and stay as the timestamped record, per this registry's append-only policy. Evidence: [`DISCLOSURE-2026-09-28-evidence.md`](DISCLOSURE-2026-09-28-evidence.md).

**June 29 primaries (document `PULSE-INTEL-PRIMARY-LOCK-2026-0629`).** The 13 calls were written in an AI-assisted research session (Claude, with web search), claude.ai chat 40b5e186-86a3-4252-a4d1-cb0497ca68af, from public polling, endorsements and news reporting, not by the PULSE INTEL model, which had no data for any of the 13 races. The document was created on 2026-06-30 at 02:22 UTC, not at its stated time of 2026-06-29 00:00 UTC, and has not been modified since; an identical copy was created at 02:48 UTC. No SHA-256 hash was ever computed: the document's verification block still reads "[PLACEHOLDER — to be computed and inserted upon final lock]". The original document published no margin band for the New Hampshire Senate Republican primary; the 8–20 point band was added later in the platform build, so the call is rescored as "winner correct, no band published". The original Google Doc is the record of truth for these calls, not the build's reworded rationales.

**May 26 general-election predictions.** The document "PULSE INTEL 2026 Race Predictions", dated 2026-05-26, was never published; it was marked for distribution to select prospects and investors, and its claim of 23 locked predictions was repeated in investor and prospect materials. It was a draft generated outside the model, in a claude.ai chat session (chat 10a7bf07-caec-4a54-af57-c480d7f6771b). Its statement that the calls were generated by the PULSE INTEL platform was incorrect. It was never hashed or locked: its verification block is blank, and it was never committed to this registry. Its 23 calls are not forecasts by any PULSE INTEL method. Withdrawn in full. For MI Sen, NC Sen, OH Sen, IA Sen, TX Sen, NH Sen, GA Sen, GA Gov and ME Sen, the v61.40 forecasts are the only registered calls.

The weights and baseline figures in `README.md` and `PRIMARY_METHODOLOGY.md` are superseded by this disclosure. The plan to lock November 2026 general-election forecasts under `v1-composite-tuned` is withdrawn. Any future methodology will be published before its first lock, with its weights taken from the code that runs it.
